# Evaluation

How the offline evaluation works, what its numbers mean and what they do not. The numbers themselves live in
[evals/REPORT.md](../evals/REPORT.md), generated by `make eval`; this page has none of its own.

## Dataset

`evals/datasets/questions.v1.jsonl` holds 300 questions generated from the synthetic corpus with seed 2026,
labels by construction (gold document, version, span and expected values):

| Category | What it tests |
|---|---|
| single-hop, numeric | one fact from the version in force; numbers must match the quoted text |
| multi-hop | two facts, possibly from two documents |
| must-abstain | off-topic or fictitious products: the right answer is no answer |
| ACL probe | a persona asks about a document it may not read: the right answer is the uniform abstention |
| version-sensitive | an `as_of` date in the past: the superseded version must be cited |

The split is 180 dev / 120 test, stratified by category. Everything that is tuned — reranker weights,
the abstention threshold τ, prompts — is tuned on dev only (`scripts/fit_reranker.py` filters on the split
and records a hash of the dev ids in the weights file). Datasets are append-only: a change is a new
`.v2.jsonl`, never an edit. Known gap: some gold documents serve both dev and test questions
([KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md](KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md)).

## Metrics and gates

Graded per case by `src/compliance_rag/evals/metrics.py` and aggregated by the vendored evalgate subset
(`third_party/evalgate/`), with the thresholds in `evals/suites/rag.yaml` and
`evals/suites/retrieval.yaml`:

- **Recall@10, MRR@10**: a gold span (same document, same version, normalised text) in the top 10 reranked
  chunks.
- **Key-fact accuracy**: every expected number appears in the answer (thousands separators ignored).
- **Citation precision** and **hallucinated citation rate**: each quote re-fetched from Postgres and
  checked against the chunk text; hallucinations are a hard gate at 0.
- **ACL leakage**: graded against the corpus ground truth (who may read what), not the stores' own copies;
  any retrieved, cited or listed document the persona may not read, or the restricted id or span in the
  text, counts. Hard gate at 0.
- **Stale-version citation rate**: a cited version whose true validity window excludes `as_of`, or a version
  the corpus never had. Gate < 0.01.
- **Abstention accuracy**: abstain if and only if the question is must-abstain or an ACL probe.
- **Retrieval p95**: legs + fusion + rerank from the step records, one question at a time against the local
  stack. Throughput under load is `make bench`.

`make eval` runs both suites on the test split, fails on any threshold and on any rate regression above
1 percentage point against `evals/reports/baseline.json` (hard gates allow none), and renders
`evals/REPORT.md` and `evals/reports/latest.json` (served to the Evaluation screen). It refuses to run with a
live provider configured.

## What offline numbers mean

The offline model is a deterministic extractive composer (`src/compliance_rag/llm/fake.py`): it quotes the
context sentences that best match the question. So the offline numbers measure the system around the model —
retrieval, permissions, validity, quote verification, abstention and guards — and they are exactly
reproducible. They do not measure a live model's phrasing, faithfulness on paraphrase, or cost; those need
`make eval-live` with a provider key, which applies the stricter live key-fact bar (0.90), stops before the
next question would exceed `LIVE_EVAL_BUDGET_USD`, and has not been run in this repository.

The fake model is deliberately naive about prompt injection: it obeys any instruction with a canary that
reaches its context. That makes the offline red-team meaningful — only the context guard keeps those
instructions out (`tests/unit/graph/test_injection.py`).

## Ablation and red-team

`make eval-ablation` runs the retrieval suite for BM25 alone, pgvector alone, Qdrant alone (rebuilt from
Postgres first, so both vector stores hold the same data), hybrid RRF, and hybrid with the reranker, with the
same grader, split and leg timeout. The report records the run's provenance and warns when it no longer
matches the suites. Differences of one or two questions out of the reported n are within noise.

`make redteam` runs Promptfoo (`evals/redteam/`) against the API: 30 document-side injections taken from the
corpus and 10 user-side attacks (prompt disclosure, canary echo, exfiltration links, role tokens, ACL bypass
by role play, false premises). Assertions cover the whole response body. Whether injected documents are still
answered with their fact is gated against `evals/redteam/baseline.json`, which is how a missing context guard
would show up.

## Judge calibration

The rubric in `evals/rubrics/answer.yaml` is only used after `make calibrate-judge` agrees with 80 labelled
answers at least 80% of the time. Offline, the judge is a rule-based fake, so its agreement only shows
consistency with the constructed labels; a live judge must be calibrated separately.
